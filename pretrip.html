Your pre-trip checklist
Work through these before your trip for the smoothest day possible
Before you arrive
0/4
Download the Disneyland app and link your tickets
Make dining reservations 60 days in advance
Check height requirements for your group members
Book parking or arrange hotel transportation
Lightning Lane strategy
0/3
Lightning Lane Multi Pass is available from 7am day-of — buy early for best ride selection
Individual Lightning Lane for Star Wars and Radiator Springs sells out fastest — have payment ready
Book new LL passes as soon as your current one is scanned, not when the window opens
Park hopper rules
0/2
Park hopping is available after 11am — you must have entered your original park first
Confirm your ticket type includes park hopper — single-day tickets typically do not
What to pack
0/5
Comfortable walking shoes — you'll average 8-12 miles per day
Portable battery pack — your phone will be working hard all day
Sunscreen, hats, and rain ponchos (Southern California can surprise you)
Refillable water bottle — free water is available at quick service locations
Small backpack or crossbody — lockers are available but slow you down
Mobile order strategy
0/3
Place mobile orders 30-45 minutes before you want to eat — arrival windows fill up fast at peak times
Set up payment in the Disneyland app before your trip to avoid fumbling at mealtime
Avoid ordering from Galactic Grill and Rancho del Zocalo at noon — extremely busy
PhotoPass & memories
0/2
Disney PhotoPass photographers are found at icon locations — scan your ticket to link photos
Memory Maker / PhotoPass+ bundle saves money if you plan to buy photos — purchase before your trip
On the day
0/3
Arrive at park gates 30 minutes before official opening for rope drop advantage
Head to back of park first — most guests rush toward the front at opening
Take a mid-afternoon break at your hotel — return after 5pm when crowds thin
