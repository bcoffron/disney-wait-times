What happens next
  • We build each of your days from park open to park close, around your must-dos, your meals, and your shows.
  • In the app you'll get live wait times, a heads-up when a wait on one of your rides spikes or drops, and park maps that work offline.
  • Your plan keeps updating as your trip gets closer — park hours, closures, and showtimes included. Tips for all of it live in the Co✦Pilot tab.
Before you go
Download the Disneyland app and link your tickets before you arrive.
Lightning Lane Multi Pass goes on sale at 7 AM each day — earlier picks are better picks.
On rope-drop days, be at the gates an hour before opening.
